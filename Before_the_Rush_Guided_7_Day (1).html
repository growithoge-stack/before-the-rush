Project your Christmas sales profit

Before you launch your Christmas sale, use this calculator to estimate how much money your campaign could bring in and what may remain after your expenses.

1. Estimate your sales

Enter the average price you expect customers to pay and how many products or orders you hope to sell. If you sell different products at different prices, use an average selling price.

2. Estimate your costs

Enter the total amount you expect to spend to fulfil the sales target above. Use the total for the whole campaign, not the cost of one item. Enter 0 if a cost does not apply.

Your projected sales, costs and profit will appear here.

This is a projection, not guaranteed income. Your result depends on achieving the sales target and keeping costs within your estimates. Use realistic figures. Include all costs you expect to pay; general monthly overheads, taxes and unexpected costs are not included unless you add them under other expenses.

Plan your discount

Test a discount before you announce it. See what the customer will pay and what may remain after the cost of that order.

Your discounted price and estimated profit will appear here.

This discount check shows the amount left after the product and order costs entered above. It does not include shared costs such as advertising, rent or salaries. Review your projected profit too when planning the whole campaign.
